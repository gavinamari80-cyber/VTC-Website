ABOUT VTC

Welcome to VTC, the premier competitive league hub for The Thrill of the Fight 2. We bring structure to VR boxing by hosting weekly numbered fight nights complete with full match cards, undercards, and main events, alongside weekly amateur bouts for fighters building up their records. VTC features official leaderboards to track standings across active championship divisions, dedicated matchmaking channels, and an active community focused on high-level competition.
